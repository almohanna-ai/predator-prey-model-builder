# Reference verification

Every reference cited in the figures and tables was checked one by one: journal articles against Crossref (title, authors, year, journal, volume, pages, DOI), with OpenAlex and web sources for missing page ranges; books and items without a DOI against library catalogues and citing sources. Content was checked against the full text for the open-access sources whose results the review uses (marked **full text read** below, with the location); otherwise against the abstract. The statements still to be checked against full texts are listed in `paper/fulltext_checklist.md`. Last update: 19 October 2026.

## Corrections made

- **T2.14**: Alves & Hilker (2017) → **Teixeira Alves & Hilker (2017)**.
- **T3.1**: gestation delay now cites Cushing's 1977 **monograph** on delay models, not his 1977 SIAM paper.
- **Φy.1**: Chaudhuri (1986) (competing species) → **Chaudhuri & Saha Ray (1996)** (prey–predator harvesting).
- **E.3**: added **Liu & Bai (2014)**; Bao et al. (2011) concerns competitive systems.
- **T1.3**: removed Brassil (2001); the additive Allee term is credited to Dennis (1989).
- **T2.12**: added Maynard Smith's result that a fixed-number refuge stabilises more than a fixed-proportion refuge.
- Bibliographic details fixed: missing co-authors (Loladze et al.), misspelled or upper-case names, incomplete page ranges.
- **Stage C additions (19 October 2026)**, corrections to the DOI registry: Gopalsamy & Weng (registry year 1991; the issue, vol. 16 no. 1, is 1993); Luo et al. (registry splits the given names "Bing Hong" and "Jin Qing"); Hilker & Lewis (registry gives the online year 2009; the issue is 2010).
