# Reference verification

Every reference cited in the figures and tables was checked one by one: journal articles against Crossref (title, authors, year, journal, volume, pages, DOI), with OpenAlex and web sources for missing page ranges; books and items without a DOI against library catalogues and citing sources. Content was checked as far as abstracts and citing literature allow; the full texts were not read.

## Corrections made

- **T2.14**: Alves & Hilker (2017) → **Teixeira Alves & Hilker (2017)**.
- **T3.1**: gestation delay now cites Cushing's 1977 **monograph** on delay models, not his 1977 SIAM paper.
- **Φy.1**: Chaudhuri (1986) (competing species) → **Chaudhuri & Saha Ray (1996)** (prey–predator harvesting).
- **E.3**: added **Liu & Bai (2014)**; Bao et al. (2011) concerns competitive systems.
- **T1.3**: removed Brassil (2001); the additive Allee term is credited to Dennis (1989).
- **T2.12**: added Maynard Smith's result that a fixed-number refuge stabilises more than a fixed-proportion refuge.
- Bibliographic details fixed: missing co-authors (Loladze et al.), misspelled or upper-case names, incomplete page ranges.
